Privacy Policy

SoloExpense (Android, package com.soloexpense) · Effective date: October 7, 2026 · Last updated: October 7, 2026

This policy explains what information SoloExpense ("the app", "we", "us") handles, where it goes, and the choices you have. The app is published by BenBen Inc, Hanoi, Vietnam, who is the controller of the limited personal data described below. Questions: vanbavlkt.56@gmail.com.

The short version
  • There is no account and no cloud backup. Your expenses, clients, projects and receipt photos are stored only on your device.
  • Receipt text is read on your device. If AI-assisted scanning is used, the receipt photo is sent to our AI processing provider to extract the amounts, and is not used for anything else.
  • The free version shows ads (Google AdMob and AppLovin), which use device identifiers. Premium removes all ads.
  • We use Google Firebase for usage analytics, crash reports and remote configuration.
  • We do not sell your expense data, and we never send your list of expenses, clients or projects to anyone.

1. Information stored only on your device

Everything you create in the app stays in the app's private storage on your phone:

  • Expenses (amount, date, merchant, category, tax, payment method, notes, billable and deductible flags)
  • Clients, projects, categories and the merchant list used to improve scanning
  • Receipt photos you capture or pick from your gallery
  • Your settings (language, currency, date format, reminders) and usage counters such as how many free scans or exports you have used this month

We have no server that receives this data, so we cannot see, recover or restore it. Uninstalling the app, or using Settings → Clear all data, deletes it permanently.

Exports and backups. When you export a PDF/CSV report or a data backup, the file is created on your device. It only leaves your device if you choose to share or save it somewhere (email, a cloud drive, another app). What happens to it then is governed by the service you choose.

2. Receipt scanning

On-device text recognition

Text on a receipt photo is read on your device using Google ML Kit. The photo and the recognised text are not sent to us. ML Kit may send Google anonymous diagnostic and performance information about the library itself (ML Kit terms).

AI-assisted scanning

To read difficult receipts (blurry, handwritten, unusual layouts), the app may use an AI vision service. Depending on the app version and configuration, this happens either automatically when on-device scanning cannot find the total, or only after you tap Use AI when asked. When it happens:

  • The receipt photo, together with your category names, a list of merchant names from your device and your default currency (to help the AI choose the right values), is uploaded to a third-party AI processing provider, which stores the image temporarily in Google Firebase Storage and processes it with an AI model.
  • A random, anonymous identifier is created so the provider can operate the service. It is not linked to your name, email or Google account.
  • The result (merchant, date, amounts, tax, category suggestion) is returned to your device. Nothing is saved until you review and save the expense.

Receipts can contain personal information, such as the last digits of a card, a store address or your name on an invoice. If you do not want a receipt processed by AI, decline the prompt or enter the expense manually.

4. Device permissions

  • Camera to photograph receipts. Optional: you can refuse and pick a photo or enter expenses manually.
  • Photos are accessed through the system photo picker, and only the image you select is shared with the app.
  • Notifications for optional reminders, such as the weekend reminder to log receipts.
  • Run at start-up (boot completed), to re-schedule your reminders and home-screen widget after the phone restarts.
  • Internet and network state for ads, analytics, AI-assisted scanning and sending issue reports.

5. Legal bases (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

  • Contract: processing a receipt with AI when you use that feature; processing Premium purchases.
  • Consent: personalised advertising and the related storage of identifiers, collected through the consent form. You can change it at any time in Settings → Privacy settings.
  • Legitimate interests: crash reporting, usage analytics and security, to keep the app working and improve it. You can object by contacting us.

6. Advertising choices

  • Change your ad consent at any time in Settings → Privacy settings.
  • Reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • Premium removes all ads.

7. Sale and sharing of personal information (US state laws)

We do not sell your expense data, receipts, clients or projects. In the free version, ad partners receive device identifiers and ad-interaction data to show and measure ads. Under some US state laws (for example California's CCPA/CPRA) this may be considered "sharing" for cross-context behavioural advertising. You can opt out through Settings → Privacy settings, by deleting your Advertising ID in Android settings, or by upgrading to Premium. We do not knowingly sell or share the personal information of consumers under 16.

8. Retention

  • Data on your device: until you delete it, clear app data or uninstall.
  • Analytics data: up to 14 months; crash reports: 90 days.
  • Issue reports: as long as needed to handle the report, and no longer than 24 months.

App data is kept in Android's app-private storage, which other apps cannot read. Network traffic to our providers uses HTTPS. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so keep your device protected with a screen lock.
